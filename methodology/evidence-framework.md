# Evidence framework

Every material observation is assigned one of four evidence classes:

| Class | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| Direct evidence | A recorded field or reproducible calculation directly supports the defined condition | State that the documented condition was identified |
| Corroborating evidence | Additional evidence supports or strengthens another observation | Support a conclusion without treating it as independently dispositive |
| Indicator | A pattern prioritizes a record for contextual review | State that the condition was flagged or requires review |
| Unknown or unverified | Required evidence was unavailable or not established | Preserve the limitation and obtain additional evidence |

## Language calibration

- **Identified:** a documented rule returned the stated condition.
- **Flagged:** an analytical rule selected the record or transaction for review.
- **Indicated:** a pattern is consistent with a risk hypothesis but requires context.
- **Requires review:** additional business, management, or documentary evidence is needed.
- **Established:** reserved for conclusions fully supported by the available evidence.

The evidence does not support conclusions about intent, misconduct, fraud, final root cause, confirmed loss, recovery, or implemented remediation.
